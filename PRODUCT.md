# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Small and medium business owners in Canada (English and French) who need reliable IT, security, and payment solutions. They want enterprise-grade service without the enterprise contractor markup. They value direct communication, fast response, and personalized attention.

## Product Purpose

A bilingual lead-generation landing page for Stitch Secure, an independent IT services provider serving the Canadian market. The page converts visitors into consultation requests by clearly communicating the four service pillars (CCTV, payment terminals, custom websites, IT support) and the value proposition: competitive pricing with personalized service.

## Brand Personality

Precise but warm. Technical but not cold. Confident but approachable. Think master tailor who also happens to secure your network — meticulous, reliable, and straightforward. The brand voice is "craftsman professionalism": no corporate fluff, no jargon theater, just clear solutions and fair prices.

## Anti-references

- Generic "cyber" aesthetics with circuit-board patterns, glowing locks, or Matrix-green code
- Stock-photo-heavy contractor sites with generic handshake imagery
- Overdesigned SaaS landing pages that feel impersonal
- Dark, menacing "security" themes that scare rather than reassure
- Any site that makes IT feel inaccessible or intimidating

## Design Principles

1. **Confidence without intimidation** — The page should project expertise and reliability without the heavy-handed "fear" marketing typical of security companies
2. **Bilingual clarity** — English and French get equal treatment; toggle feels native, not bolted on
3. **Trust through transparency** — Clear service descriptions, honest pricing language, real value propositions
4. **Crafted simplicity** — Every element earns its place; no decoration without purpose
5. **Responsive first** — The site works beautifully on a restaurant owner's phone, a shop manager's tablet, and an office desktop

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.1 AA minimum
- Bilingual interfaces must keep toggles clearly labeled and translations complete
- No hover-only interactions
- Respect reduced motion preferences
